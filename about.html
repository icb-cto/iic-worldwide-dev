Visionary Leadership

Dato Dr. Arshad Mahmood

Founder & Chairman, International Investment Consortium (IIC)

Dato Dr. Arshad Mahmood is the Founder and Chairman of the International Investment Consortium Inc. (IIC). Established in Hong Kong in 1997, IIC has an ever expanding presence in Asia, Middle East, Africa and Europe. Its subsidiaries and business platforms form the foundation for growth in many existing and emerging market sectors.

Dato Dr. Arshad provides the strategic vision that drives the organization’s evolution into a global commodity, trade, and finance platform. Under his leadership, IIC has been repositioned to open markets, bridge supply and demand, and deliver financial solutions that enable legitimate trading across borders. His commitment to building trusted marketplaces and connecting producers with buyers has defined IIC’s new direction.

Dato Dr. Arshad holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Economics, a Master’s Degree in Literature, and an Honorary Doctorate in Management Sciences from ACMIT USA. His multidisciplinary academic foundation, combined with decades of experience in international trade and finance, reflects his dedication to building transparent, accessible, and efficient commodity markets.

Guided by a philosophy of creating markets where none exist, Dato Dr. Arshad advocates for connecting those who have commodities and services with those who need them. His vision emphasizes trade finance, verified supply chains, and market infrastructure that empowers producers, buyers, and suppliers to trade with confidence, contributing to economic growth across emerging and established markets alike.

Chairman’s Message

IIC Worldwide exists to open markets and create the connections that make trade possible.

We are building a platform where producers, buyers, sellers, and suppliers can find each other, transact with confidence, and access the financial tools they need to grow. Our role is to bridge supply and demand across commodities and services worldwide.

By combining commodity trading infrastructure with trade finance solutions, we ensure that legitimate businesses can access the markets they need. Whether it is agricultural commodities, energy resources, or industrial materials, IIC Worldwide provides the platform, the verification, and the financing to make every transaction secure, transparent, and efficient.

Our Identity

1997

Established in Hong Kong

The Bridge Between Supply & Demand. IIC Worldwide connects producers, buyers, and suppliers through a trusted commodity trading and finance platform, opening markets and enabling legitimate trade worldwide.

IIC Worldwide is a Dubai-headquartered commodity, trade, and finance platform that connects those who have commodities and services with those who need them. The platform serves as a marketplace where producers, buyers, sellers, and suppliers come together to trade with confidence.

Through its network of subsidiaries, affiliates, and global partners, IIC operates across agricultural commodities, energy resources, industrial materials, and international trade. The platform provides verified supplier networks, trade facilitation, and the financial infrastructure needed to move goods and capital across borders efficiently.

Rather than simply connecting parties, IIC Worldwide actively bridges the gap between supply and demand. The platform verifies trading partners, structures trade finance solutions, coordinates logistics, and ensures that every transaction meets the highest standards of legitimacy and compliance.

Its leadership combines deep expertise in commodity markets with an understanding of trade finance and regional market dynamics. This enables IIC to open new markets, facilitate cross-border trade, and provide the financing solutions that allow businesses of all sizes to participate in global commerce.

IIC Worldwide’s ambition is to become the world’s most trusted platform for commodity trading and trade finance, creating markets where they are needed, connecting legitimate trading partners, and financing the growth of global commerce.
