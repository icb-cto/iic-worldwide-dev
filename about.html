Visionary Leadership

Chairman — Dato Dr. Arshad Mahmood

Chairman, International Investment Consortium Worldwide (IIC)

Dato Dr. Arshad Mahmood is the Founder and Chairman of the International Investment Consortium Inc. (IIC), where he provides the strategic vision that drives the organization’s global growth and diversification. Through his leadership, IIC has evolved into an international platform with interests spanning investment, finance, technology, natural resources, and sustainable development. His commitment to innovation, strategic partnerships, and responsible business practices has strengthened IIC’s presence across multiple regions and industries.

Dato Dr. Arshad holds a Bachelor’s Degree in Economics, a Master’s Degree in Literature, and an Honorary Doctorate in Management Sciences from ACMIT USA. His multidisciplinary academic foundation, combined with decades of business leadership, reflects his dedication to excellence, continuous innovation, and sustainable economic progress.

Guided by an asset-based investment philosophy, Dato Dr. Arshad advocates for creating long-term value through investments backed by tangible assets and sustainable business models. His vision emphasizes responsible growth, resilience, and economic empowerment, enabling IIC to develop impactful ventures that generate lasting value for stakeholders while contributing to the advancement of communities and industries worldwide.

Chairman’s Message

IIC Worldwide is driven by a commitment to create meaningful progress through responsible investment and international collaboration. We work to support economic development, strengthen communities, and unlock opportunities that generate lasting value.

By bringing together investors, public institutions, industry leaders, and local partners, we help shape initiatives that respond to real market and community needs. Our approach is guided by transparency, innovation, and accountability, with careful consideration for the economic, social, and environmental impact of every project we support.

Dato Dr. Arshad Mahmood, Chairman

International Investment Consortium Worldwide (IIC)

Our Identity

A Trusted Global Investment Partner

IIC Worldwide develops and manages strategic investments across critical industries, creating long-term value worldwide.

IIC Worldwide is a Dubai-headquartered investment group that develops and supports opportunities across a broad range of international industries and markets.

Through its subsidiaries, affiliates, and global partners, the Group participates in both established and emerging sectors. Its activities include energy, mining, commodities, and international trade, as well as blockchain, digital assets, and technology-driven financial services.

Rather than operating solely as a source of funding, IIC Worldwide takes an active role throughout the investment process. The Group helps identify opportunities, design commercial structures, coordinate strategic partners, and guide projects from early-stage development through implementation and long-term operation.

Its leadership brings together international business experience and an understanding of local market conditions. This combination allows the Group to navigate different regulatory frameworks, respond to changing commercial environments, and pursue opportunities while maintaining disciplined governance, risk management, and compliance practices.

IIC Worldwide’s long-term ambition is to establish a resilient and diversified investment ecosystem that supports business growth, contributes to regional development, and generates sustainable value for its partners and stakeholders.
