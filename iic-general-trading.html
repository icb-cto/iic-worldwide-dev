About Us

About IIC General Trading

IIC General Trading is the commercial trading and supply-chain division of the International Investment Consortium (IIC), providing integrated procurement, sourcing, logistics, and international trade solutions.

Our business connects producers, manufacturers, suppliers, and buyers through a reliable global network designed to simplify cross-border trade.
