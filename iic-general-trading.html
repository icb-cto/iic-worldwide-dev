Who We Are

About IIC General Trading

IIC General Trading LLC-FZ is an international commodity trading company headquartered in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We specialize in the sourcing, trading, and supply chain management of commodities across energy, agriculture, metals, fertilizers, and industrial sectors.

As a subsidiary of the International Investment Consortium (IIC) Worldwide, we leverage a global network of producers, suppliers, and buyers to facilitate reliable and transparent trade across international markets.

Our operations are built on strong industry relationships, deep market knowledge, and a commitment to delivering quality products and services to our clients worldwide.

What We Do

International Trading Solutions

IIC General Trading provides comprehensive trading services across the full commodity supply chain, from sourcing and procurement to logistics and market development.

Global Sourcing

Identifying, verifying, and connecting with reliable producers, manufacturers, and suppliers across international markets. We source commodities from origin countries with established quality standards and certifications.

International Trading

Facilitating secure, compliant, and efficient cross-border commodity transactions. We manage trade negotiations, contract structuring, documentation, and execution with full transparency and competitive terms.

Supply Chain Solutions

End-to-end supply chain management including procurement, quality inspection, logistics coordination, warehousing, freight forwarding, customs clearance, and last-mile delivery across multiple regions.

Market Development

Identifying new market opportunities, developing trade routes, and establishing commercial relationships in emerging and established markets. We help connect supply to demand across growing economies.

How We Work

Our Trading Approach

A structured, five-step process ensures every transaction is executed with precision, transparency, and reliability.

1

Market Analysis & Planning

2

3

Trade Negotiation & Structuring
