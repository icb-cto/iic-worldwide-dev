About Us

About IIC Express Gold and Diamond

IIC Express Gold and Diamond is committed to building a sustainable and transparent precious metals industry by integrating mining operations, refining capabilities, and international trading under one organization.

Our expertise spans the entire value chain—from exploration and extraction to refining, valuation, and global distribution—allowing us to provide reliable and secure solutions for our partners worldwide.

With operations across multiple regions, we focus on responsible resource development, compliance with international standards, and long-term partnerships built on integrity and trust.
