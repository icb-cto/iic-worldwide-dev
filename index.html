What We Do

A Platform Built for Global Trade

We open doors to global markets — connecting producers, buyers, sellers, and suppliers across borders to create new trade opportunities where they are needed most.

We streamline commodity sourcing and supply — making it easier for businesses to find what they need, from agricultural products and energy to industrial goods and professional services.

We bridge through finance — providing structured funding, trade credit, and financial solutions that remove barriers and enable transactions between buyers and sellers worldwide.

We ensure legitimacy at every step — vetting suppliers and buyers so that every transaction on our platform is conducted between verified, trustworthy trading partners.

We deliver end-to-end supply chain solutions — from sourcing and logistics to distribution and delivery — ensuring commodities move efficiently from origin to destination.

We build the technology platform that powers transparent trading — providing digital tools for market discovery, transaction management, and real-time visibility across the trade lifecycle.

Subsidiary Network

An Integrated Global Ecosystem

IIC Worldwide brings together a group of focused subsidiaries working across finance, energy, trade, aviation, commodities and digital technology — serving markets around the world.

IIC Bank

A financial institution delivering international banking, structured funding, and digital financial services. The bank supports cross-border transactions, investment activity, and wider access to financial solutions across East Africa and other developing markets.

IIC Metaverse

A technology-focused platform developing blockchain solutions, virtual environments, and emerging digital finance products. It creates new ways for individuals and institutions to participate in digital economies and technology-enabled investment opportunities.

IIC Oil & Gas

An integrated energy business supporting trading, infrastructure, and service requirements throughout the oil and gas sector. Its operations emphasize reliable delivery, efficient execution, responsible development, and internationally recognized health, safety, and environmental practices.

IIC General Trading

An international trading and supply-chain company connecting buyers and suppliers across commodities, industrial materials, and commercial goods. It provides coordinated sourcing, logistics, distribution, and delivery solutions while maintaining strong operational and compliance standards.

IIC Aviation

A specialized aviation investment, financing, and leasing platform serving airlines and aviation-sector partners. It supports fleet growth, aircraft acquisition, asset management, and tailored financial structures in expanding regional markets.

IIC Holding

A regional investment and project-development platform supporting strategic initiatives across South Sudan and East Africa. It coordinates capital allocation, corporate governance, and long-term ventures designed to stimulate economic activity and create value within local communities.

IIC Express Gold & Diamond

A Dubai-based precious metals and high-value commodities company engaged in the sourcing, trading, and secure movement of gold, diamonds, and related assets. Its activities are guided by responsible sourcing practices, regulatory compliance, and dependable logistics.
