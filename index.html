Who We Are

International Investment Consortium Worldwide

IIC Worldwide is a global commodity, trade, and finance platform headquartered in Dubai. We open markets by connecting producers, buyers, sellers, and suppliers — creating the infrastructure for legitimate, transparent commerce across borders.

Our platform bridges supply and demand across key commodity sectors including food, energy, services, and technology. Whether a business needs to source rice, secure fuel, or find a verified service provider, IIC Worldwide connects them with legitimate, vetted partners ready to trade.

We bridge through finance — providing the trade finance, structured funding, and financial solutions that make transactions possible. By removing financial barriers and enabling access to capital, we help businesses of all sizes participate in global trade.

Through our integrated platform, IIC Worldwide creates markets where none existed before — bringing together verified trading partners, financial infrastructure, and supply chain solutions to drive economic growth and deliver lasting value for all stakeholders.

Market Access

Trade Facilitation

We streamline commodity sourcing and supply — making it easier for businesses to find what they need, from agricultural products and energy to industrial goods and professional services.

Trade Finance
