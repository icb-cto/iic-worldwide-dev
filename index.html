Who We Are

International Investment Consortium Worldwide

IIC Worldwide is a global investment and development group headquartered in Dubai. We bring together capital expertise and strategic partnerships to unlock transformative opportunities and drive sustainable growth across key markets.

With operations across global markets, IIC Worldwide is structured around specialized business sectors, including energy, mining, digital assets, financial services, and global trade. Each operates independently while aligning with a unified investment strategy focused on long-term value and sustainable growth.

We connect global capital with local opportunities by combining investment, industry expertise, and strategic partnerships. Through responsible investment and operational excellence, we develop businesses that meet real market needs and create lasting value.

By integrating investment with operational delivery, IIC Worldwide provides end-to-end solutions that drive growth, reduce risk, and generate sustainable outcomes for partners, governments, and stakeholders.
