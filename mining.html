Division Overview

Responsible Resource Development

The Mining division of IIC Worldwide focuses on the sourcing, development and commercialisation of mineral resources across Africa and other emerging markets.

Operating across the mining value chain, the division is involved in exploration partnerships, extraction operations, commodity trading and supply chain management. Its approach is built on responsible resource development, ensuring that projects deliver both economic value and local impact.

The division works closely with governments, local operators and international partners to structure projects that are commercially viable and operationally sustainable. This includes supporting infrastructure development, logistics and export frameworks.

IIC Worldwide's mining activities are positioned within high-demand global markets, with a focus on gold that supports industrial growth, energy transition and long-term supply stability.

The division presents opportunities for strategic investment, joint ventures, and long-term commodity supply agreements.
