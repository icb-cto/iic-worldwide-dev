IIC Express Gold & Diamond Opens New Trading Facility in Dubai

A state-of-the-art precious metals trading and verification facility has been inaugurated, reinforcing Dubai's position as a global gold hub.

IIC Editorial Team August 18, 2026 Dubai, UAE

IIC Express Gold & Diamond has officially opened its new trading and verification facility in Dubai, marking a significant milestone for the company and the broader IIC Worldwide network. The facility is designed to support the full lifecycle of precious metals trading — from sourcing and authentication through to secure storage and international distribution.

A Modern Trading Hub

Located along Sheikh Zayed Road, the new facility features advanced assaying and verification equipment, secure vaulting capacity, and dedicated trading floors. The investment reflects IIC Express Gold & Diamond's commitment to operating at the highest international standards for precious metals handling and compliance.

The facility has been designed to accommodate growing demand from international buyers and sellers seeking access to Dubai's established precious metals marketplace. It includes dedicated spaces for client consultations, product verification, and documentation processing.

This facility represents our commitment to building world-class infrastructure for precious metals trading. Dubai is the natural home for this investment — a global crossroads where producers and buyers meet.

Responsible Sourcing at the Core

A key feature of the new facility is its integrated responsible sourcing framework. Every transaction processed through the facility undergoes verification against international compliance standards, including origin documentation, chain-of-custody tracking, and anti-money laundering protocols.

IIC Express Gold & Diamond has adopted the LBMA's Responsible Gold Guidance as a benchmark for its operations, ensuring that all gold passing through its facility meets internationally recognised standards for ethical sourcing and environmental responsibility.

Expanding Market Access

The new facility is expected to significantly increase IIC Express Gold & Diamond's capacity to serve international markets, particularly in the Middle East, Africa, and South Asia. By combining physical trading infrastructure with IIC Worldwide's trade finance capabilities, the company is positioned to offer end-to-end solutions for precious metals transactions.

The opening marks the latest in a series of strategic investments by IIC Worldwide aimed at strengthening its position across high-value commodity markets and building the infrastructure needed to support legitimate, transparent global trade.
