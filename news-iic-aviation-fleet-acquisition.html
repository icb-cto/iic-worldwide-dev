IIC Aviation Announces Strategic Fleet Acquisition

IIC Aviation secures a fleet of cargo aircraft to support commodity logistics across Africa and the Middle East, strengthening integrated supply chain capabilities.

IIC Editorial Team June 28, 2026 Dubai, UAE

IIC Aviation has announced the acquisition of a fleet of dedicated cargo aircraft, marking a transformative step in IIC Worldwide's ability to control and optimise commodity logistics across its key operating regions. The fleet will be based at Dubai's Al Maktoum International Airport, with operational routes spanning East Africa, West Africa, and the Indian subcontinent.

Closing the Logistics Gap

For many commodity traders operating in emerging markets, unreliable and expensive logistics represent one of the most significant barriers to growth. Perishable agricultural commodities, high-value precious metals, and time-sensitive trade documents all require transportation infrastructure that much of Africa and South Asia currently lacks.

IIC Aviation's fleet acquisition addresses this gap directly. By operating dedicated cargo routes, the consortium can guarantee delivery timelines, reduce transit costs, and maintain chain-of-custody integrity for sensitive cargo — capabilities that are particularly critical for precious metals and agricultural commodities requiring cold chain management.

Logistics is the last mile of trade finance. You can have the capital, the buyer, and the commodity — but without reliable transportation, the deal doesn't close. IIC Aviation closes that gap.

Integrated Supply Chain

The fleet acquisition completes a critical link in IIC Worldwide's integrated supply chain model. Commodity transactions facilitated by IIC General Trading and financed by IIC Bank can now be physically delivered through IIC Aviation, creating a seamless end-to-end service that reduces counterparty risk and improves transaction certainty.

The aircraft fleet includes both wide-body freighters for bulk agricultural cargo and smaller, secure-configured aircraft for precious metals and high-value consignments. All aircraft will be equipped with real-time tracking and temperature monitoring systems.

Route Network

Initial routes will connect Dubai with Nairobi, Kampala, Lagos, and Dar es Salaam, with expansion to Addis Ababa, Lusaka, and Mumbai planned for early 2027. The route network has been designed to align with IIC Worldwide's existing trading corridors, ensuring maximum utilisation and commercial viability from launch.

IIC Aviation expects to begin scheduled operations by September 2026, with charter services available immediately for consortium members and verified trading partners.
