IIC Bank Launches Digital Trade Finance Platform

IIC Bank introduces a fully digital trade finance platform, enabling faster approvals, real-time tracking, and seamless cross-border transactions for commodity traders.

IIC Editorial Team July 22, 2026 Dubai, UAE

IIC Bank has unveiled a comprehensive digital trade finance platform designed to modernise how commodity traders access financing, manage documentation, and track cross-border transactions. The platform represents a significant step forward in the consortium's mission to make trade finance more accessible, transparent, and efficient.

Digitising the Trade Finance Lifecycle

The new platform digitises the entire trade finance workflow — from application and credit assessment through to disbursement and settlement. Traders can submit financing requests online, upload supporting documentation, and receive credit decisions within 48 hours, compared to the industry average of two to three weeks for traditional paper-based processes.

Real-time dashboards provide complete visibility into transaction status, outstanding balances, and repayment schedules. The platform supports letters of credit, documentary collections, pre-export financing, and supply chain finance products.

Digital infrastructure is the foundation of modern trade finance. By removing friction from the process, we enable traders to focus on what they do best — moving commodities from where they are to where they are needed.

Built for Emerging Markets

The platform has been specifically designed to address the challenges faced by commodity traders in Africa, the Middle East, and South Asia — regions where the trade finance gap is most acute. Mobile-responsive interfaces ensure accessibility even in areas with limited desktop infrastructure, while offline capability allows document preparation without continuous internet connectivity.

Multi-currency support, automated compliance checks, and integrated KYC verification reduce the administrative burden on traders while maintaining the rigorous standards required for international commodity transactions.

Integration with the IIC Ecosystem

The digital platform integrates seamlessly with IIC Worldwide's broader trading operations. Traders using IIC General Trading or IIC Express Gold & Diamond for physical commodity transactions can access financing directly through the platform, creating an end-to-end solution that combines physical logistics with financial services.

IIC Bank plans to extend the platform's capabilities throughout 2026, including the addition of blockchain-based document verification and smart contract settlement for qualifying transactions.
