IIC Worldwide Expands Trade Finance Operations Across East Africa

The consortium announces a strategic expansion of its trade finance capabilities, strengthening commodity supply chains across key East African markets.

IIC Editorial Team September 1, 2026 Dubai, UAE

IIC Worldwide has announced a significant expansion of its trade finance operations across East Africa, marking a strategic milestone in the consortium's mission to bridge supply and demand in emerging commodity markets. The initiative focuses on providing structured financing solutions to producers, buyers, and intermediaries engaged in cross-border trade within the region.

Strengthening Regional Trade Infrastructure

The expansion targets key markets including Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, and South Sudan, where demand for structured trade finance continues to outpace available supply. By deploying capital and expertise into these markets, IIC Worldwide aims to reduce friction in commodity supply chains and enable legitimate businesses to access the financing they need to grow.

Through its subsidiary network — particularly IIC Bank and IIC General Trading — the consortium is building an integrated approach that combines financial products with physical commodity logistics and verified supplier networks.

Our expansion into East Africa reflects our core belief: where there is legitimate trade, there should be accessible finance. We are building the bridges that connect producers with markets and capital.

Key Focus Areas

The trade finance expansion encompasses several strategic priorities. Pre-export financing for agricultural commodity producers enables farmers and cooperatives to secure working capital ahead of harvest seasons. Letters of credit and documentary collections provide payment assurance for cross-border transactions, reducing risk for both buyers and sellers.

Supply chain financing programmes are being developed to improve cash flow throughout commodity value chains, from primary production through to final delivery. These programmes are designed to be accessible to small and medium-sized enterprises that have historically been underserved by traditional banking infrastructure.

Looking Ahead

IIC Worldwide plans to establish dedicated trade finance desks in Nairobi and Kampala by the end of 2026, staffed by professionals with deep regional expertise and supported by the consortium's global commodity network. The initiative represents a long-term commitment to building sustainable trade infrastructure in one of the world's fastest-growing economic regions.

The expansion aligns with IIC Worldwide's broader strategy of creating markets where they are needed, connecting legitimate trading partners, and providing the financial tools that enable global commerce.
