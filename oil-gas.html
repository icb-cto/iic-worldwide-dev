Oil & Gas Trading

Operating across the global energy supply chain, providing trading, logistics and infrastructure support for refined petroleum products and energy solutions.

Division Overview

A Reliable Partner in Complex Energy Markets

With a strong regional presence in the Middle East, Africa and South Asia, the division focuses on the sourcing, distribution and delivery of fuel products to industrial, commercial and government clients. Its operations are supported by established supplier networks, logistics capabilities and quality assurance processes aligned with international standards.

Beyond trading, the division is actively involved in energy-related project development, including infrastructure, storage and engineering support services. This enables the business to move beyond transactional supply and into long-term energy solutions.

The division is positioned as a reliable partner in complex and high-demand environments, where consistency of supply, operational efficiency and regulatory compliance are critical.

Commercially, the Oil & Gas vertical offers opportunities across long-term supply agreements, infrastructure partnerships, and integrated energy solutions aligned to regional growth markets.
