Mining

Sector Overview

A Vital Global Industry

Mining is a vital global industry that supplies the raw materials needed for infrastructure, manufacturing, energy, transportation, technology, and everyday life. Minerals and metals support a wide range of sectors, from construction and electronics to renewable energy, communications, and advanced industrial production.

As demand for mineral resources continues to grow, the industry is evolving through technological advancement, automation, improved safety practices, environmental management, and more responsible use of natural resources.

The global transition toward cleaner energy and more digital economies has also increased the strategic importance of minerals such as lithium, copper, nickel, cobalt, graphite, manganese, and rare earth elements.

IIC supports the responsible development of the mining sector by bringing together industry expertise, technology, infrastructure capability, operational coordination, and strategic partnerships.

Across the Full Value Chain

The extraction, processing, and refinement of high-value metals used in jewelry, investment products, technology, and industrial applications.

The production of metals that are fundamental to construction, manufacturing, transportation, infrastructure, and industrial activity.

  • Tin mining
  • Nickel mining

Mining Infrastructure

  • Mine-access roads
  • Rail and transport infrastructure

  • Power-generation and distribution systems

Mineral Processing and Beneficiation

Operational practices and technologies designed to reduce environmental impact, improve resource efficiency, and strengthen long-term sustainability.

Our Approach

Integrated Resource Development

IIC takes a broad, operational view of the mining industry. We recognise that successful resource development depends on more than extraction alone. It requires strong governance, reliable infrastructure, technical capability, environmental planning, efficient logistics, skilled personnel, and responsible engagement with local communities.

Responsible Resource Management Operational Safety & Efficiency Environmental Protection Technology & Innovation Local Employment & Skills Transparent Governance Infrastructure Reliability Long-Term Community Value

IIC is committed to contributing to a modern, responsible, and future-ready mining sector. Our activities span the full mining ecosystem, from exploration and resource assessment to extraction, processing, infrastructure, technology, environmental management, and rehabilitation.

By combining industry knowledge, operational capability, strategic coordination, and international partnerships, we aim to support mining operations that strengthen supply chains, contribute to industrial growth, improve resource security, and create lasting value for businesses, communities, and economies.
