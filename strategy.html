Smart Investment, Lasting Results

We invest with purpose — putting capital to work across key sectors and growing markets to create real, long-term value.

Strategic Agenda

70%

Of the capital needed for each project, typically put in by IIC

Connected Businesses, Sustainable Growth. IIC Worldwide takes a clear and focused approach to investment — working across multiple sectors, partnering with the right people and building businesses that deliver strong returns over time.

The Group builds connected investment platforms that bring together financial strength and hands-on delivery — making sure every project is set up to succeed commercially while also making a real difference in the communities where we operate.

(01/04)

Capital Deployment

A top priority for the Group is growing our presence across Africa, the Middle East, Asia, and Europe by investing in infrastructure, energy and digital technology where the opportunities are strongest.

IIC typically puts in 70% of the capital needed for each project, helping reduce risk and get things moving. As the project grows and local partners prove their capability, ownership gradually shifts in their favour. The goal is simple — build businesses that can stand on their own long after our initial investment is complete.

(02/04)

Cross-Sector Integration

IIC Worldwide connects its businesses — finance, trade, energy, aviation and technology — so they work together as one coordinated system, creating more value than any single unit could alone.

In practice, this means building large-scale projects that bring together production, processing, energy, logistics and community services into one self-sustaining model. IIC provides the investment plan, strategic direction and global know-how, while local partners and workers handle the day-to-day delivery — keeping economic value rooted in the communities where we invest.

(03/04)

Geographic Expansion

IIC Worldwide is active across Africa, the Middle East, Asia, and Europe — focusing on fast-growing markets where our investment can deliver strong commercial results and make a real difference to local communities.

Every time we enter a new market, we do it with a plan — creating jobs, supporting local businesses and building long-term economic strength. We bring the capital and the strategy; local partners, entrepreneurs and workers deliver the results on the ground.

(04/04)

Strategic Partnerships

IIC works closely with governments, institutions and private companies to deliver important projects across infrastructure, agriculture, energy, healthcare and education.

These partnerships go beyond money — IIC brings in the best technology from around the world, connecting projects with top expertise in areas like agricultural technology, renewable energy, digital systems and smart logistics. The result is a model that works commercially while genuinely serving the communities it is built for — creating lasting value for people, not just investors.
